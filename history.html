Past Games

Twenty-two games. The same six mechanisms.

Every FTC game is new and every FTC game rhymes with an old one. Study the last five seasons and you will recognize the intake, the lift, the launcher, and the climb the next time kickoff drops them on you in a new costume.

What repeats

Almost every year

Pick up a thing, put it somewhere higher

  • Skystone, Freight Frenzy, PowerPlay, CENTERSTAGE, INTO THE DEEP.
  • Intake plus lift plus release.
  • The robot you should be able to build in your sleep.
Every 3–4 years

Launch a ball or ring

  • Velocity Vortex, Ultimate Goal, DECODE, BIOBUZZ.
  • Flywheel with velocity control, an indexer, and vision for aiming.
  • Consistency beats range.
Most years

Endgame climb or hang

  • Rover Ruckus, CENTERSTAGE, INTO THE DEEP.
  • Hooks on slides, a ratchet, and the courage to start it with 25 seconds left.
Recurring

Sort or identify by color

  • Rover Ruckus minerals, INTO THE DEEP samples, DECODE artifacts.
  • A color sensor in the intake and a camera for the field.
Recurring

Randomized autonomous

  • Relic Recovery pictographs, DECODE's MOTIF, most games' randomized start.
  • Vision (now AprilTags) decides the auto path.
Recurring

Human player

  • Freight Frenzy, PowerPlay, INTO THE DEEP, DECODE, BIOBUZZ.
  • Feeding the robot is a skill.
  • Practice it like driving.

How to study a past game

  1. Watch the animation, then read the scoring section of that season's manual (archived at ftc-resources).
  2. Watch a Worlds finals match and count cycles and the time spent on endgame. That is what "good" looked like.
  3. Find three reveal videos from teams with different strategies. Sketch each robot's intake, lift, and end effector.
  4. Search the FTC Discord showcase for that game's name to find CAD and portfolios.
  5. Write one page: what the winning archetype was, and which of the six patterns above the game used. Do this for the last five games and kickoff will feel familiar.
